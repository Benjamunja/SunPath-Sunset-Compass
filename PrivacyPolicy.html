Privacy Policy for SpotGenic

Last updated: August 2026 · App: SpotGenic (formerly Sunset Compass / SunPath) · Operator: Benjamin Harris

SpotGenic ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Location Data

  • What we collect: Your device's precise GPS location (latitude and longitude), when you grant permission.
  • Why: To show nearby photo spots, calculate sunset/sunrise/moon directions and timing, overlay sun paths on the map, and save location pins you create.
  • Storage: Processed on-device. Saved pins (including coordinates) are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly use a future cloud-backup feature.
  • Third-party sharing: We do not sell your location. Location may be passed to map providers solely to render the map around you (see Third-Party Services below).

Camera and Photo Library

  • What we collect: Photos you choose to capture or select when creating or editing a location pin.
  • Why: So you can attach images to photo-spot pins and share visual location cards.
  • Storage: Images associated with pins are stored locally on your device (and in any share export you initiate).
  • Permission: Camera and photo library access are optional; denying them limits pin-image features only.

Advertising and Tracking Data

  • What may be collected: Advertising identifier / IDFA (where permitted), device information, and ad interaction data.
  • Why: To show advertisements via Google AdMob for free (non-premium) users.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): On iOS we request tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the advertising identifier for personalised ads.
  • Opt-out: Limit ad tracking in device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: relevant Google ad settings). Purchasing Premium / ad-free removes in-app ads.

Purchase and Subscription Data

  • What we collect/process: Purchase status, subscription entitlements, and restore-purchase results via Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat.
  • Why: To unlock premium (ad-free) features and restore purchases across devices.
  • Note: Payment card details are handled by Apple or Google, not by SpotGenic. RevenueCat processes entitlement status according to their own privacy policy.

Device Information

Our third-party service providers — Google AdMob (advertising) and RevenueCat (purchases) — may collect standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version as part of their normal operation (ad serving, entitlement checks, and fraud prevention). This is governed by their own privacy policies, linked below. SpotGenic does not currently operate its own separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Shared Links

When you share a pin, a link may include coordinates and optional pin metadata (name, category, notes). Recipients who open the link see that information on a web landing page and/or in the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • Calculate and display sun/moon timing and direction for your location
  • Show maps, compass, planner, and AR visualisation tools
  • Save and share photo-location pins (with optional images)
  • Deliver AdMob ads to free users
  • Manage premium entitlements via RevenueCat and the app stores
  • Improve app performance and reliability

Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: Pins, settings, and related app data are stored on-device (e.g. AsyncStorage / app sandbox).
  • No SpotGenic user accounts: The app does not require creating an account with us.
  • Cloud backup: Any cloud backup feature (if enabled in a future release) would be optional and disclosed at the point of use.
  • Security: We use standard platform security practices for on-device data.

Your Rights and Choices

  • View and delete saved pins in the app
  • Revoke location, camera, photo library, and tracking permissions in system settings
  • Limit ad tracking as described above
  • Purchase Premium to remove ads
  • Restore purchases through the store / in-app restore flow

Children's Privacy

SpotGenic is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have collected such information.
